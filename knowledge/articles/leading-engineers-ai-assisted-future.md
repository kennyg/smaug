# Leading your engineers towards an AI-assisted future

## Summary

Strategy guide for engineering leaders on adopting AI-assisted development. Addresses the common pressure from executives about AI adoption rates and provides a practical approach to driving meaningful improvements while building organizational support.

## Key Concepts

- **Overall Approach**: Lead with experimentation, communicate clear direction using metrics, provide organizational support
- **Addressing CEO Pressure**: Move beyond hype metrics (like "50% code written by AI") to meaningful productivity improvements
- **Strategy Components**:
  - Experimentation phase to understand AI capabilities and limitations
  - Clear communication using appropriate metrics
  - Organizational enablement and support structures
