# OSSU Computer Science - Path to a Free Self-Taught Education

Complete undergraduate-level computer science education using online materials, designed for self-directed learners seeking a well-rounded grounding in fundamental computing concepts.

## Overview
OSSU provides a structured curriculum equivalent to a CS degree's core requirements, curated from the world's best universities (Harvard, Princeton, MIT, etc.) but freely available online.

## Curriculum Structure
1. **Intro CS** - Try out CS and determine if it's right for you
2. **Core CS** - First three years equivalent, required courses for all majors
3. **Advanced CS** - Final year equivalent, electives based on interests
4. **Final Project** - Validation and consolidation of knowledge

## Key Features
- All or nearly all material is free (some courses charge for graded assignments)
- Can be completed in ~2 years with 20 hours/week dedication
- Spreadsheet available for timeline estimation
- Peer review for final projects
- Active community support

## Evaluation Criteria
Courses must:
- Be open for enrollment
- Run regularly (ideally self-paced)
- Meet CS 2013 Curriculum Guidelines standards
- Be of high quality in teaching materials and pedagogy

## Community & Support

- Active contributor and community base
- Written in HTML
- Based on Open Source Society University initiative

## Prerequisites
Assumes students are already educated outside CS field.
